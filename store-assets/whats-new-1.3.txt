New in this update:

• Work on a document together: share a link and edit at the same time, with live cursors, names and chat. Invite-only, and you approve who joins
• Print from the app, through Android's printer: documents, spreadsheets, and PDFs at their real quality rather than as pictures
• Open CAD drawings (DWG and DXF): pan and zoom, switch layers on and off, and print just the area you mark out
• Settings shows which build is running, with a check for updates
• "Open with" no longer loses the file you opened to recovered work from last time

Still fully offline unless you choose to share, and nothing is stored on a server.
