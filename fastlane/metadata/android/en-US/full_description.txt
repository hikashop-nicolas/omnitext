Omnitext opens practically any file you are sent, in one app. Instead of installing a separate app for each file type, often slow, with ads, upgrade banners, a mandatory account, or uploading your file, you get the right viewer or editor for each file: documents, spreadsheets, PDF, code, maps, subtitles, audio, video, archives and more. It opens quickly, saves back without breaking what it did not change, and never gives up on a file: anything unknown opens as text or hex.

It is a private, offline editor that adapts to whatever file you open: code and data formats (JSON, YAML, XML, CSV, Markdown and more) in a proper editor, and PDF, Word (.doc/.docx), OpenDocument (.odt) and spreadsheets (.xlsx/.ods) in dedicated editors, all on your device.

Everything runs locally. Your files are never uploaded, there is no account, no tracking, and no ads. Open a file, edit it, and save it back, entirely offline.

<ul>
<li>Edit PDFs: change text in place, add text and images, pinch to zoom.</li>
<li>Edit Word (including legacy .doc), OpenDocument and spreadsheets, preserving the parts you do not touch.</li>
<li>Spreadsheets with charts, pivot tables, Power Query, conditional formatting, printing, freeze and split panes, form controls and VBA macros that run.</li>
<li>Code and data editor with syntax highlighting for many formats, and a tree editor for JSON and YAML.</li>
<li>Edit subtitles (SRT, VTT, ASS and more) and play video and audio with subtitles, including Dolby audio tracks and Apple Lossless.</li>
<li>Edit maps (GeoJSON, KML, GPX) and view many more formats: PowerPoint, ebooks, 3D models, fonts, SQLite databases, email, archives (zip, 7z, RAR) and images, with on-device text recognition.</li>
<li>Command palette, light and dark themes, and version history to roll back changes.</li>
</ul>

Private by design: your files never leave your device unless you share a document, and then it goes straight to the people you invite. The optional AI features (text recognition, translation, writing help, transcription) download their model once, and ask before they do.

Free software under the MIT licence. The same app also runs in any browser.
